**Search for Santa** is a _Snacker Tracker_ adventure themed around Christmas and New Year's Eve celebrations! 

The Ministry of Snacking discovers that one of Santa's workers has been **secretly giving out snacks**! All the blue gifts contained snacks, for years, while snacking is **strictly forbidden**! Now Hunter, the best Snacker Tracker in the world, must find out **who did it** and put a stop to it. If she doesn't, the Ministry will cancel Krysmas celebrations forever ...

After the success of _Day of the Bread_, enjoy another **puzzle story** full of wonder, humor, adventure, and clues to figure out.

## Snacker Trackers

What's the Snacker Tracker universe of stories? Well,

* **It's a puzzle story.** It's a proper children's novel. At the start, however, are **32 beautiful portraits of suspects**. As you read, you get clues about the thief and can eliminate suspects, until by the end only one remains ...
* **It's educational.** A tense and fun story navigates the reader through the traditions and culture associated with these celebrations. The main character uses logic, math, and language clues to figure out who did it.
* **Chapters are short and language is simple.** You can read the book _to_ your Apprentices, but it was written to suit the kids that start reading books entirely on their own (and older).
* **Colorful and funny.** The book has a beautiful design inspired by Day of the Dead. It's full of color and illustrations. The _story_ itself has color and humor too.

The puzzle is a so-called "elimination puzzle". It's one of the easiest to explain/play, so great for kids, but also very visual and interesting. You get portraits from the characters in the book, as well as some basic information like their name or hobbies. Clues can be a simple discovery like "their name contains an A" or something a bit harder to spot in later chapters ...
