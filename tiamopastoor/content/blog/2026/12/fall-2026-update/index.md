Welcome to my update article for Fall 2026. It is an overview of the work I've done, projects I've finished, and any other interesting developments during the months of October, November, and December 2026.

When I started doing these update articles, they were very long and detailed and specific. I guess I was also working harder back then and hopping around between projects more, but I also simply documented the process more and really tried to keep a diary to sort my thoughts.

Now, a few years later, I feel like I've changed as a person and my process has changed. I write half of these updates at the _start_ of the season now (like a to-do list for the next 3 months for myself), and then finish the other half at the _end_ of the season (simply remembering what I ended up doing). I don't actually update it during the season, I don't actually write down detailed thoughts about the work done. This makes the articles far less useful and more like "oh dang it's the 31st I have to write that article now, let's do it quickly!"

Also, years ago I used to work on longer projects. Books that took a month. Video games that take even longer. Musical albums. Those are projects that last a season and are nice to talk about. In recent times I've mostly been making tiny project after tiny project for my [educational online store](https://thewaywardmelody.com). Some of these take only a single day from start to finish, thanks to their size but also my experience and the process/automation I've set up. That's not worth talking about in these updates.

{{% example %}}
For example, I've built a really advanced and useful quizzing system and have always been very good at researching and learning new information. I can easily gather hundreds of different questions on a topic and turn them into a professional finished (educational?) quiz within a day. I certainly wouldn't be able to do that if I did not have my system and my processes set up for it.

So ... I think these monthly updates will disappear next year. I'll just do one update for the whole year.

Having said that, let's do a final quarterly update for 2026!

## What was the plan?

Take random tiny steps every day to grow the online store and fill more of the curriculum. Whenever possible, if my brain _does_ decide to get excited by something new, then give myself a week or so to divert my attention and do _that_. But only if it's small enough.

I have many bigger ideas. Some are ambitious and experimental, but some are just that: not risky, but big. I am most excited about those ideas and I truly believe some of them will be very succesful. But I've seen the proof, over and over, that right now my health sucks too much for those big projects. My brain is so incredibly busy that I can barely finish a thought or develop an idea before the next one presents itself. What (dis)interests me changes daily, if not hourly. I have basically been "delaying" my dream projects for 10 to 15 years, making much tinier projects to fill the time ... until I can finally be treated and improve my health.

Hopefully, this season (or the next) that treatment can finally start. And then I can go for bigger projects which will become better but most importantly lead to some sustainable income.

Not a very specific plan, I'll admit. But that's what it is.
